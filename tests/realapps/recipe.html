Lemon loaf

Serves 8 · 15 min prep · 50 min in the oven

You need

  • 225 g flour
  • 175 g sugar
  • 175 g soft butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons, zest and juice

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour and the lemon zest.
  4. Bake for 50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mix the lemon juice with 85 g sugar and pour it over the warm loaf.
