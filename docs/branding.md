# Branding: Reach and the services it connects to

Reach shows which brain is driving a task. Claude and ChatGPT are other companies' products and trademarks. This page records what their published rules allow and what Reach does. Read on 2026-11-24; Anthropic's guidelines re-read on 2026-11-25 (unchanged).

Footer used in the README and in Settings (About):
> Claude is a trademark of Anthropic. ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of OpenAI. Reach is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.

## OpenAI (ChatGPT)

Sources:
- Design guidelines: https://openai.com/brand/ (the public page; the full guide at brand.openai.com needs a sign-in, which we didn't do)
- Logo pack: https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip (OpenAI Blossom and wordmark, black and white, SVG and PNG)

What the rules say (short):
- Use the logo only when it directly relates to OpenAI services. Use it exactly as provided and acknowledge it belongs to OpenAI.
- Don't stretch, crop, recolour, add effects, or use unapproved variations. Don't add colours to the Blossom.
- Don't use the Blossom as primary branding, over a busy image, or more prominently than your own name or mark. Give it the prescribed spacing and "lots of open space".
- Don't imply sponsorship, endorsement or affiliation. Don't put the logo into your own branding, and don't design a similar logo.
- An app's name, logo, description, slogan, tagline and screenshots should be your own and free of OpenAI's brands. Don't use "GPT" or model names in product names.
- Wording: not "collaborated with", "worked with", "partnered with" or "building with". Allowed: "building on OpenAI", "developed on GPT-4". Say clearly that the product is independent and not affiliated, endorsed or sponsored.
- OpenAI can change the guide or end the permission at any time. Changes must be made within a reasonable time.
- No minimum size or clear-space number is on the public page. We use the SVG as supplied, which already has empty space around the mark (about a quarter of its width on each side), and never draw it smaller than 22 px including that space.

What Reach does:
- No Blossom anywhere. ChatGPT appears as the word "ChatGPT" only, with a small neutral status dot in the model picker, like Claude.
- README, gallery and social-preview images are free of OpenAI's brands, as OpenAI asks of an app's screenshots.
- Reach's own logo is a pointing hand reaching toward a dot, with no tile: near-black (#111) on light surfaces, white on dark ones; the fixed exe and installer icon is white with a thin dark outline. It shares nothing with the Blossom's shape, and Reach's name and logo are always bigger than any service mark.

## Anthropic (Claude)

Sources:
- Trademark guidelines: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines (effective 2024-08-01)
- Press kit: https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit (zip with the Claude logo, the Claude icon, the Claude Code logo and the Claude spark)

What the rules say (short):
- "You may only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand." Permission can end at any time.
- Anthropic supplies the images and the size and spacing rules. No changes to colour, font or proportion.
- No use that implies sponsorship, endorsement, a relationship or an affiliation. No use that could hurt Anthropic's reputation.
- Keep a clear background and reasonable space around the mark. Don't add a trademark symbol.
- Trademarks include names, logos and "brand elements".
- For use in a product, email marketing@anthropic.com (their page says this is for businesses with an existing relationship).

What Reach does:
- No Claude logo, icon or spark anywhere, because nothing has been approved. Claude appears as the word "Claude" only, which says which service is connected.
- The pointer and tag don't take Claude's clay orange either: colour counts as a brand element, and using it would suggest a link with Anthropic.

## The pointer colour per brain

The idea was multiplayer-style colours: the pointer and tag take the brain's own colour. We didn't do it:
- Claude's colour is an Anthropic brand element, and using it needs their approval (above).
- OpenAI's marks are black and white only. A black pointer disappears on dark screens, and the Blossom rules forbid adding colours.

So both brains use Reach's own cobalt blue, with the same shape. While it thinks, the tag shows the brain's name as a word ("Claude", "ChatGPT"), never a mark.
